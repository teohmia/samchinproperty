ABOUT SAM

Your business partner in real estate.

Sam Chin represents Huttons Asia (CEA Reg. R008856D, Agency Licence L3008899K) with more than a decade of experience in Singapore's private residential market. He specialises in landed house sales and rentals, condominium leasing and commercial property, with a particular focus on Districts 2, 9 and 11.

Beyond transactions, Sam is known for producing 360° virtual tours for his listings and for carrying out new-home defect rectification inspections — helping clients move into freshly completed units with confidence.
